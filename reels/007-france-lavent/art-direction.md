# Art direction: "Les Français ne se lavent pas ?" (reel 007)

Same visual language as reel 001 (see its art-direction.md): Anton + Inter, ink #0A0A0C, bone #F4F1EA, red FAUX stamp only, sand-gold accent #E3A72F. Typographic and diagrammatic only, no people.

- Signature device (climax only): "Comme leurs voisins." with two equal gold bars (France / voisins).
- Anti-patterns: no berets, baguettes, Eiffel Tower, accordion, French flag clichés. No bathroom imagery.
- Scene primary subjects (all different): 1 stereotype + stamp, 2 two equal bars, 3 century card + crossed hot water, 4 two-word pairing, 5 Europe to France label, 6 timeline XIXe-XXe, 7 final line + source.
